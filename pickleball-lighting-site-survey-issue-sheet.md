# Pickleball lighting site survey issue sheet

Use this sheet with the RFQ when one or more fixture locations still need a site check. Give each location a reference and send the same revision to every bidder.

## Indoor mounting measurements

Record support elevation, the luminaire reference height used in the model, horizontal coordinates and the lowest part of the installed assembly. Mark overhead obstructions and access routes on the drawing. Ask the responsible site team to confirm permissible attachments. The [indoor pickleball lighting mounting survey checklist](https://aihuafeili.com/indoor-pickleball-lighting-mounting-survey/) explains the fields.

## Price the unresolved scope visibly

If a location is still assumed, ask the bidder to state the mounting hardware and access method included in the price. Put any unresolved allowance on its own line. When the survey closes the item, record the revised drawing and whether the calculation, hardware or installation price changes. The [14-fixture indoor pickleball lighting quotation case](https://aihuafeili.com/indoor-pickleball-lighting-quote-14-fixture-case/) shows how one specified fixture schedule connects to an equipment and installation comparison.

## Close the issue

Keep the survey response with the accepted fixture schedule. Record who approved the location and which document supersedes the assumption. This gives the installer and venue team the same set of references at handover.
